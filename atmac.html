ATMAC

Year: 2020

Participating project at the CtrlZ.AI zine fair supported by Google

A DIY pop-up ethics council for advanced technologies

The Advanced Technology Makeshift Advisory Council (ATMAC) is a DIY pop-up ethics council that is easy to replicate and open to everyone. The aim is to foster critical public discourse around algorithmic systems and AI applications. Its name hints on Google’s failed AI ethics committee ATEAC - Advanced Technology External Advisory Council, from 2019.

ATMAC was developed for the CtrlZ.AI Zine Fair, organised by members of Google’s ethical AI team. It took place as an off-site companion event alongside the 2020 ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency (FAT).
