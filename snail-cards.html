I’m a Vienna-based designer and researcher working on access in different forms: to spaces, to information, to materials. In my work I combine design methods, artistic strategies and creative research techniques, speculative design approaches and visual communication. My projects range from neuro-inclusive interventions and mental health awareness to developing situated relationships to material resources in urban space.

My practice has been shaped by work at renowned creative studios such as Superflux in London, where I contributed to films on the tangled networks of global technological infrastructure, and at The Invisible Lab in Graz, where I did design research, graphic design and UX/UI design for clients addressing groups of people with varying accessibility needs.

I develop and facilitate educational formats such as walks, workshops and talks for universities, festivals and conferences, including Urbanize! Festival, the University of Applied Arts Vienna, the CtrlZ.AI fair in Barcelona and the Wuhan Institute of Technology.

Snail Cards

Year: 2026

Initially designed for the exhibition opening for “Neurotopias” at Improper Walls, Vienna.

Communication cards to support neurodivergent users in navigating social events

Communication badges offer greater accessibility to events, conferences and university to those who may struggle with non-verbal social cues. Since their first recorded use in 1995 during an Autism related conference, they have become widely adopted at Autistic-run events, or those with a high number of Autistic visitors.

For my design I used a silicone lanyard to ease possible sensory irritation oftentimes caused by fabric lanyards due to their rough edges or seams. The cards can be easily flipped without the need to unclip them.

The cards can be used at conferences, events, meetings or other social events. Please get in touch if you are interested in purchasing them or otherwise working together on a related project.
